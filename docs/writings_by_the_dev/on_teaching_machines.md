Intro
A Teaching Machine guides a learner toward the discovery of a concept, while a Learning Machine preserves the intricate patterns and relationships formed during this educational dialectic. The process of education seeks to ingrain a pattern of concepts in the student's mind, which, once established, ideally facilitates the ability to acquire deeper and more complex concepts. These, in turn, allow for the advancement of specific skills or understanding. Regardless of the goals dictating which concepts are chosen for imprinting, the ultimate objective is always to reproduce an understanding of a suite of concepts in the learner. Essentially, any education system is a Teaching Machine: a set of products (concepts understood, in this case) that are the desired outcomes, and a series of steps that, if performed in the correct sequence, lead to these products being produced, meaning the concepts are understood by the learner. All machines can be refined and improved, to this ambition how might we achieve a higher education system? What is the ultimate teaching machine?
Each concept we acquire dwells in a unique sector of our cognitive modeling space, bounded by a suite of distinct knowledge fragments arranged in a temporal, spatial, and hierarchical fashion. Each fragment exists as a concept in its own right which illustrates the hierarchical nature where concepts are building pieces for more abstract ideas. A new concept resolves its presence in this substrate once enough knowledge fragments coalesce, aligning with a common reference point and observed within a scope that allows a comprehensive view of their interconnected relationships. Learning involves mapping these fragments within our cognitive terrain, necessitating an optimally tailored sequential delivery and rhythm of information for maximizing the ease of constructing each mental model. The strategic curation of educational content enables the intuitive comprehension and integration of modular elements into a broad internal model, the periphery of which delineates a concept's comprehensive scope.
As we navigate this path of discovery, we identify knowledge fragments as independent concepts themselves, contributing to a layered, multi-dimensional intellectual architecture. This dynamic framework negates the need to relearn established concepts in the case when multiple distinct notions rely on the same sub pieces for its construction. Instead, pointers expediently summon them for integration into broader mental schemas. Resembling nested "Russian dolls," each concept, once identified,  is readily accessible for insertion into grander constructs, its 'address' serving as a positional reference within the model, much like data entries in a spreadsheet. This system allows an elaborate aggregation of knowledge, with each stratum interconnected through a web of relational pointers, embodying a unified, cohesive, and accessible repository of amassed knowledge. From this substrate we construct everything we come to know or imagine.

LLMs
Recent breakthroughs in artificial intelligence, particularly in Large Language Models (LLMs), signify a transformative epoch in our pursuit of the ideal teaching apparatus. Engaging in dialogue with an LLM through a conversational interface has refined the crude integration between the corpus of human knowledge and the mind's innate inclinations for absorbing information. ChatGPT, emblematic of this innovative fusion, stands at the frontier of at the intersection of LLM technology and user interfacing, though it is not without competition. Numerous contenders are aggressively ensuring the momentum of innovation at a pace that oscillates between exhilarating and daunting.
Despite the remarkable opportunities this technology has already enabled, there exists subtle but critical friction points that curtail its full educational potential from being realized. I believe these are possible to smooth out, significantly amplifying the current LLM’s utility without the need for any additional underlying model performance improvements. Merely a refinement to our method of interfacing with this language companion could catapult our educational capabilities forward significantly.
More advanced LLM development is ongoing, rapidly iterating and improving. Each incremental improvement in model sophistication undoubtedly acts as a vector towards a higher teaching machine. Progress in the direction of a stronger underlying model architecture likely has a very long runway ahead of it. However, I posit that the current bottleneck hindering a transformative leap in teaching efficacy is our mode of interfacing with these systems. The tech breakthroughs have already happened and no more are needed for this to become reality. All that is needed is the shaping of the current capabilities to more approximately cater towards our intuitive curiosity pursuits. It's within the subtle art of adjustment that the promise of a more capable teaching machine lies—not as a distant prospect, but as an accessible transformation available today limited only by the development of greater cognitive ergonomics.

New UI
In its present form, as we engage in a dialogue with LLMs like ChatGPT, we encounter a unique challenge: maintaining the thread of curiosity without becoming entangled in a maze of tangential questions. During these engagements, the responses often prompt additional queries before we've fully processed the initial reply. When a particular point piques our interest, the instinct is to delve deeper into that specific aspect, which can instigate a recursive loop of questions and sub-questions. This necessity to scroll past relevant material to chase emerging curiosities interrupts the natural progression of understanding and risks scattering our original focus. Each layer of inquiry takes the user deeper into a specific vein of curiosity but increasingly distances them from the overarching context they originally sought to understand. Yet, it is precisely within these meandering paths that deep learning often occurs. It is at this intersection where the integration of a more dynamic interface could revolutionize how we traverse through the conceptual landscapes of knowledge.
The potential for branching inquiries at any juncture of the exploration highlights the critical problem that needs to be solved. Pursuing one line of questioning inevitably leaves the exploration of others in its wake, leading to a scattered and disorganized dialogue. The inherently chronological nature of the chat interface does not cater to an organic, multi-directional exploration of a topic. The user, wishing to maintain the integrity of the original inquiry, finds themselves caught in an ever-growing tangle of chat logs.
Upon returning to the original message to continue where they left off, the user is confronted with further questions, each new inquiry adding to the spatial disconnection from the original context. This growing fragmentation contributes to a cognitive burden, elevating the effort required to continue the exploration and risking the abandonment of the intellectual pursuit.
Addressing this user experience challenge calls for a reimagined interface, one that supports the divergent nature of human curiosity. A solution could be the implementation of a non-linear, graph-based interface that allows users to branch out from the main path of inquiry and explore sub-questions without losing sight of the central thread. This interface could resemble a mind map, where each node represents a key point in the LLM's response, and each branch represents a sub-question or a topic expansion. The users can navigate through this knowledge graph, expanding and collapsing branches as they explore, maintaining an organized overview of their learning trajectory.
The envisioned interface would not only track our exploratory routes but also offer the ability to delve deeper into any subtopic without losing the original context. Imagine a digital workspace where each point of interest can expand into a dedicated sub-chat, much like opening a folder within a folder on a computer, each leading to finer details. This would allow learners to seamlessly toggle between macro-overviews and micro-examinations, creating a digital memory of individual and collective cognitive patterns.
Such an interface could allow for the creation of 'curiosity trees', where the root represents the initial query and each branch a follow-up question or related topic. Users could then visually track the paths they have taken in their learning journey and seamlessly switch between different lines of inquiry without the need to scroll through a lengthy chat history.
By dynamically connecting questions and their contextual roots, this approach reduces cognitive load and aligns with the way humans naturally explore and understand complex information. It transforms the learning experience from a linear exchange into a dynamic exploration, effectively bridging the gap between human curiosity and the digital information landscape.
This capability would not merely serve as a convenience; it would mirror the intricate workings of our minds. Just as our neurons form complex networks, with each memory or piece of knowledge linked to multiple others, the digital interface could visually and functionally replicate this interconnectedness. By doing so, the LLM becomes more than an information source; it transforms into a cognitive extension, facilitating a level of organization and retention of knowledge that transcends traditional learning methods.
Yet, the true power of such a system lies not only in accommodating individual explorations but also in its collective utility. As users carve their paths through various subjects, these routes could be preserved, analyzed, and optimized by the AI. This collective intelligence could then guide newcomers, offering a multitude of paths—some direct, others scenic—each adapted to different learning styles and objectives.
Moreover, as this knowledge ecosystem evolves, it could become predictive, suggesting exploratory routes that enhance understanding based on the user's past interactions, current goals, and even the less-traveled paths that hold untapped insights. This is the next frontier of personalized education, where each question asked adds a stone to the edifice of human knowledge, and each answer found paves the way for others to follow.
In essence, we are not just building a tool for learning; we are crafting a dynamic repository of human curiosity and intellect—a digital Athenaeum where each contribution enriches the collective and empowers the individual. This is the promise of AI in education, not as a static repository of information but as an active, evolving partner in the quest for knowledge, the ultimate teaching machine.
