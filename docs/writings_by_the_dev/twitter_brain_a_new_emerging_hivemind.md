Likely making their information exchange more adopted by the collective crowd than a less connected node. Even if the viewpoint isn't widely adopted the hive reaches a higher consensus based on the information exchanged and the sharing node contributed even if its input was disagreed with. Systems behaving as a hive mind are very prevalent. I believe they exist in some form anywhere there is a shared network of thought. There are many formations of hive minds, crypto as I mention, religion, sports fans, etc. One however, I find myself contemplating the most is political affiliations. If you associate with the left or right you will be connected to a different tree of mostly downward propagation of information. There are nodes where information is shared to its connecting nodes but the connecting nodes don't also communicate back to the sharing node. Depending on the hive mind you belong to, you will become aware of a different collection of knowledge and arguments for values. There are many beliefs WE ALL  hold purely because of the hive(tribe) we associate with. Our information accuracy and breadth is at the whim of our upstream nodes.
I do not believe it is possible to ever exit the hive influence and be a purely independent thinker. I expect it may actually be a disadvantage to be in this situation. If the information is real and useful, having a shared network of information exchange can be very advantageous to the participants. Of course it can be a detriment also if the information is noise rather than signal. So there is a competitive advantage of belonging to a hive that exchanges and processes information in the most accurate way. A hive that finds accurate actionable meaning in the information that is shared should be able to make better decisions than a hive that propagates falsehoods. It seems intuitive to me there is a competition happening as to which hive can collect information about the world, affording greater agency to act based on the info. If there is less signal or even false signal there should be opportunity to be out-competed by an information network that just more accurately understands the nature of reality. Additionally, the nodes (people) most capable of rationalizing may tend to realize the traits of the superior hive and migrate to it. Further feedback looping into its advantage over other hives. In layman's terms, the hive with the most accurate info and highest quality nodes to process the information will win.
