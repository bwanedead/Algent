On Concepts - What is a concept and how do we understand things
What is a concept? This intuitively understood fragment of the human experience, the general understanding of a cohesive way of viewing the dynamics of a particular resolution and content of semantics. In short it's what you think of when you are thinking about something. When I say shoe and either you have a sensation of understanding or relating to a particular consideration of the universe this is your concept of a shoe. You might have a mental image and be able to cycle through variations that encompass this concept. See a red shoe with a white stripe down the side, a white leather cap to the toe end of the shoe, maybe a grid of ventilation pores. Now turn this to a brown shoe, now black, a skater shoe, converse, a hiking boot, keep cycling the details and representation that embody the concept of a shoe. 

Each individual representation of a shoe that you conceive is an individual concept in itself. The concept is essentially a shoe in that way, and maybe we give that particular form (pattern of details) as an air jordan or yeezy. But somehow these are all unified under a framing and resolution to considering the universe. There is a hierarchical structure to our concepts, they lead into others, and are utilized as foundations for building towers of abstractions. A concept is foundational to the degree another one depends on the comprehension of the previous in order to achieve comprehension. Each concept is a pattern of relationships between details, and at every resolution of detail there exists a conceptual framing. Where a concept is essentially the range of details and their relationships you are able to consider together. Each continual zoom, widening, pan across the field of details that you conjointly understand a pattern of relation between is a different conceptual frame in which you are interpreting. From this you can see how there is a space of overlap between each individual concept but yet each technical minute shift in details or relationship pattern has to be considered as a unique construct. Where often when the overlap is sufficient there is a practicality to viewing them as the same unless some practical use relies on the minute difference as to the details being considered. 

For example, if my conceptual structure of a shoe (the pattern of details that all collectively imply a shoe) is different than yours, we can still meaningfully discuss shoes unless our conversation starts pertaining to if a shoe is or isn't something that includes a particular detail. It is an aggregate consideration of a field of details and relationships. Where Concepts (particular clusters of relationships) can become details themselves for higher abstract concepts. And considering or forming the understanding of any concept you can conceive is equivalent to being aware of a pattern of relationships between details. The concept is the entire stack of details you are actually capable of tracking and keeping a record as to a trail of relationships. 

You might notice this hinges on the meaning of what a detail is. So let's describe what a detail represents. A detail is delineating a separation of a particular substrate as being 2 separate things. When you place a line in the universe as one side being different from the other in some dimension of measure. A detail simply is where you find importance in distinguishing that 2 sides of a dimension coordinate are different from each other. Our details are separating forms of the universe into distinct parts and our concepts are when we track a pattern of how these separation points relate to each other. 

We can then continue to apply this method in a nested hierarchical way where the more we assign concepts as being details themselves and building concepts on top of concepts we expand into abstraction and can see higher level form to the program we are in.
